# Chronic Condition Management & Lifestyle Support

## 1. Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)

- Daily blood pressure readings should ideally be taken at the same time each morning before morning medications.
- Maintain sodium intake under 2,000 mg/day (DASH diet pattern).
- Moderate physical activity (e.g., 30 minutes of brisk walking 5 days a week) improves vascular elasticity.

## 2. Type 2 Diabetes

- Coordinate insulin or oral hypoglycemics with meal timing to avoid hypoglycemia.
- Hypoglycemia symptoms: shakiness, sweating, rapid heartbeat, confusion. Rule of 15: consume 15g fast-acting carbs (e.g., 4 oz fruit juice), wait 15 minutes, re-check glucose.

## 3. Mental Wellness & Breathing for Chronic Illness

- Chronic illness and medication routines can elevate stress and anxiety.
- Daily breathing exercises (e.g., Box Breathing, 4-7-8 Calm pattern) activate the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering heart rate and blood pressure while improving adherence discipline.
