# Chronic Condition Management & Lifestyle Guidance

## 1. Blood Pressure & Cardiovascular Wellness

- Daily blood pressure readings should ideally be taken at the same time each morning before morning medications.
- Maintain sodium intake under 2,000 mg/day (DASH dietary pattern).
- Moderate physical activity (e.g., 30 minutes of brisk walking 5 days a week) supports vascular elasticity and heart health.

## 2. Blood Glucose & Metabolic Wellness

- For individuals prescribed blood glucose therapies: coordinate doses with scheduled meal times to maintain glycemic stability.
- Hypoglycemia awareness: recognize symptoms such as shakiness, sweating, rapid heartbeat, or lightheadedness. Standard rule of 15: consume 15g fast-acting carbohydrates (e.g., 4 oz fruit juice), wait 15 minutes, and re-check levels.

## 3. Mental Wellness & Breathing for Medication Routine Support

- Managing daily medication routines can sometimes elevate stress or anxiety.
- Daily breathing exercises (e.g., Box Breathing, 4-7-8 Calm pattern) activate the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering resting heart rate while improving daily adherence discipline.
