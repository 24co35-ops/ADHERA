# Medication Adherence & Routine Best Practices

## 1. Daily Routine & Timing

- **Consistency**: Taking medications at the same time each day helps maintain steady therapeutic blood levels.
- **Pairing with Habits**: Pair your dose with an established daily anchor (e.g., brushing teeth in the morning, evening meal, or bedtime).
- **Food Requirements**:
  - Some medications require food to prevent stomach upset or enhance absorption (e.g., Metformin, NSAIDs).
  - Other medications must be taken on an empty stomach (e.g., Levothyroxine: 30–60 minutes before breakfast with a full glass of water).

## 2. Missed Dose Protocol

- **General Rule**: If you miss a dose, take it as soon as you remember.
- **Close to Next Dose**: If it is almost time for your next scheduled dose, skip the missed dose and resume your normal schedule.
- **Never Double Up**: Do NOT take two doses at once to make up for a missed dose unless specifically directed by your physician.

## 3. Safe Medication Storage

- Keep medications in their original labeled containers.
- Store in a cool, dry place away from excessive heat, moisture, and direct sunlight (avoid bathroom medicine cabinets due to shower humidity).
- Keep out of reach of children and pets.

## 4. Managing Travel & Timezone Shifts

- Keep medications in your carry-on luggage with original prescription labels.
- For short trips (1–3 days) crossing timezones, consult your care provider on whether to adjust dose timing or maintain home timezone intervals.

*Disclaimer: This guide provides general adherence information. Always follow your prescribing physician's personalized instructions.*
